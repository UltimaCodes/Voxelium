# M11 — shaders (forward HDR pipeline)

date: 2026-07-22

> Record of the milestone, not of the current engine. Two things have moved since: shadows
> are **PCSS** with a blocker search, not the PCF 3x3 below, and the pipeline has gained
> SSGI, TAA, motion blur, distance fog, clouds and a day/night cycle. `SPEC.md` Tier 3 is
> the current state.

full pipeline per frame: shadow cascades -> HDR scene -> SSAO -> composite (AO +
volumetric) -> bloom down/up chain -> ACES + sRGB. every effect has a live HUD toggle
and a measured cost.

## what got built, in spec order

1. **cascaded shadow maps** (render/ShadowMap, shadow.vert/.frag). 3 cascades in a depth
   texture array, splits {26, 80, 240}. each cascade fits a fixed-radius bounding sphere
   of its frustum slice and snaps the center to shadow texels in light space using
   *double-precision* world coords — shadows don't swim when the camera moves or rotates.
   PCF 3x3, normal-offset bias scaled by cascade texel size (no acne, no peter panning on
   axis-aligned faces). front-face culling on casters. cascades are culled per-cascade
   through the same cull.comp (ortho box = 6 planes now, perspective view still 5) so the
   near cascade draws a handful of chunks instead of the whole world.
2. **SSAO** (ssao.frag), half res, 12 samples. view position reconstructed from the
   reversed-z depth, flat normals from derivatives (exact on voxel faces). 4x4 box blur
   over the rotation noise, then multiplied onto the scene in the composite pass.
3. **water**: deferred — the test world has no water yet. the transparent pass + water
   shading slots in cleanly when a water block exists; noted, not faked.
4. **volumetric sun scattering** (composite.frag): 12-step raymarch through the shadow
   cascades along the view ray, jittered to hide banding, forward-scattering phase.
   gated by the shadows toggle (it samples the cascades).
5. **bloom** (bloom_down/up.frag): Jimenez 13-tap downsample + 9-tap tent upsample, 5
   mips. soft-knee threshold (1.15) on the first tap only, so bloom picks up sun
   highlights and glow blocks instead of hazing the whole frame; added, not mixed.
6. **ACES + exposure + sRGB** (final.frag): Narkowicz ACES fit, manual sRGB encode so
   output is identical across drivers. HDR throughout (RGBA16F), exposure slider.
7. TAA: stretch goal, skipped.

lighting model in chunk.frag: warm directional sun (2.05,1.62,1.08) gated by NdotL x
shadow x voxel-sky-access, cool dim sky ambient, warm torch from block light. the
sun/ambient contrast is what keeps it from going milky after tonemapping.

## costs (GPU queries, 1280x720, radius 10, RTX 2070S, --smoke benchmark)

| stage | cost |
|---|---|
| base scene (no post) | 0.71 ms |
| shadows (3 cascades, cull + draw) | +5.12 ms |
| ssao | +0.21 ms |
| volumetric | +0.24 ms |
| bloom | +0.23 ms |
| **full pipeline** | **6.02 ms** |

honest notes:
- shadows dominate and are vertex-bound: dropping the maps 2048->1536 barely moved it
  (5.35->5.12), so it's primitive throughput (up to ~3M quads drawn 3x), not fill. the
  real lever is shadow-caster LOD / distant chunk simplification, which is a proper
  future optimization, not a milestone-11 tweak. per-cascade culling already took it
  from 9.44 -> 5.12 ms by not drawing the whole world into every cascade.
- 6 ms at radius 10 with everything on. the spec's < 4 ms target is at radius 32 and will
  need the shadow LOD work above; with shadows off the whole pipeline is ~0.9 ms.
- byte-for-byte identical terrain shading before/after the per-cascade shadow cull —
  correctness proof that culling dropped only chunks outside each cascade box.
