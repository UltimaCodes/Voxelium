# M8 — cave culling (tommo's advanced cave culling)

date: 2026-07-21

## what got added

- `mesh/Connectivity`: per-chunk face-to-face visibility. explicit-queue BFS over air
  voxels, seeded only from the chunk's border planes (interior bubbles can't connect
  faces so they're never even visited). every air component ORs the faces it touches
  into 15 face-pair bits. computed in the mesh job on workers, alongside the mesh.
  uniform fast paths: all-air = all 15 bits, all-solid = 0.
- visibility flood in ChunkManager: BFS outward from the camera's chunk. steps from
  chunk to chunk only through face pairs the chunk's air actually connects, and never
  back toward the camera (per-path direction mask — that's what bounds the flood; it
  can over-cull wrap-around paths, the algorithm's documented trade). chunks visited
  once, first path wins. unmeshed chunks count as fully connected — never cull what
  isn't known yet.
- recomputed only when the camera crosses a chunk boundary, the chunk set changes, or
  the toggle flips. steady-state per-frame cost: zero.
- result feeds the gpu as a 1 KB bitset (persistent-mapped, indexed by cull-meta slot);
  cull.comp tests it right before the frustum planes. HUD checkbox toggles it live.

## numbers (radius 10, --smoke)

| scenario | chunks drawn | vs frustum-only |
|---|---|---|
| above ground, standard camera | 203 | 349 → **42% fewer** |
| buried in solid stone | **3** | 231 → **98.7% fewer** |

the above-ground image stayed **byte-identical** (773,776 px) while drawing 146 fewer
chunks — the flood removed only geometry that was provably occluded. that's the
correctness proof and the win in one measurement. underground is the headline: 3 vs
231. with real caves (M10) this is the difference between rendering a mountain's worth
of hidden rooms and rendering the tunnel you're standing in.

cost: mesh-job avg rose 0.05 → 0.21 ms (connectivity BFS included). still 5x under the
1 ms budget and it runs on workers. if it ever matters, the flood can be rewritten
over the mesher's existing solidity bitmasks (64 voxels per op) instead of per-voxel
BFS — noted, not needed.
